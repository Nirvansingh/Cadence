================================================================================
02. FEATURES & STUDENT/LEARNER WORKFLOW SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================
Project: Next-Gen Open-Source Productivity Suite & Hybrid SaaS
Target Audience: High School Students, Self-Taught Coders, Knowledge Workers

1. THE HABIT LOOP TRACKER & DAILY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built upon the neuroscience of "The Habit Loop": Cue ➔ Routine ➔ Reward.

Core Components:
  - Daily Interactive Checklist:
      * 1-click round circular checkbox with smooth spring-physics tick animation.
      * Instant sound feedback and micro-haptic visual reward.
      * Live Daily Progress Ring: SVG circular progress showing completion (e.g. 71%).
      * Streak Flame Engine: Tracks consecutive completion days with flame badges (🔥 5d).
  - The Habit Loop Blueprint Drawer:
      * Clicking "The Habit Loop ▾" on any habit reveals its 3 connected steps:
          1. ⚡ Cue (The Trigger): Time, context, or preceding action.
             Example: "Alarm rings at 6:00 AM" / "Walking home from tuition at 4:00 PM".
          2. 🔄 Routine (The Action): The micro-step to execute.
             Example: "Drink 500ml water + stretch" / "Solve 5 Math NCERT problems".
          3. 🎁 Reward (The Benefit): The psychological celebration.
             Example: "Zero morning fatigue" / "Guilt-free 1h evening gaming".
  - 7-Day Mini Heatmap Row:
      * Visual 7-dot strip (M T W T F S S) showing weekly consistency at a glance.
  - Category Tiers:
      * 🌅 Morning Ignition (Wake up, hydrate, plan day).
      * 📚 Deep Study & Homework (Tuitions, homework, revision).
      * 💻 Coding & Creation (Building tools, shipping software).
      * 🏃 Physical Health (Movement, pushups, walks).
      * 🌙 Evening Wind-down (Pack school bag, formulas review, sleep).

2. ADAPTIVE TIMETABLE & CALENDAR ENGINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Designed specifically for modern students with fragmented days (school, commutes,
tuitions, self-study, coding, meals, sleep).

Features:
  - Day-by-Day Schedule View (Monday to Sunday):
      * Each day has its own independent, customizable schedule.
      * Pre-populated with intelligent templates (School + Tuition, Self-Taught Dev, Exam Sprint).
  - 24-Hour Hairline Timeline:
      * Slim visual bar representing the full 24 hours of the day.
      * Segments color-coded by category (School, Tuition, Study, Workout, Meals, Sleep).
      * Dynamic Free Time Calculator: Instantly computes remaining discretionary hours.
  - Commute & Transit Buffer Automation:
      * Built-in 10-15 minute buffers for freshening up, packing bag, or transit.
  - Export & Sync:
      * 1-Click offline .ics calendar generator formatted with RFC 5545 compliance
        (Asia/Kolkata or user timezone, weekly recurrence RRULEs).
      * Single-click import into Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar.
      * Optional direct Google Calendar API integration.

3. DEEP FOCUS & POMODORO STUDIO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features:
  - Customizable Interval Engine:
      * 25m Focus / 5m Break (Classic Pomodoro).
      * 45m Focus / 15m Break (High School Class Block).
      * 90m Deep Work (Ultradian Rhythm Coding Sprint).
  - Precision Stopwatch with split laps.
  - Full-screen Zen Mode: Distraction-free countdown timer with minimal UI.
  - Ambient Sound Synthesizer: Procedural White Noise, Brown Noise, Rain Sounds
    (100% offline, zero streaming lag).

4. SCRATCHPAD & STUDY NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features:
  - Local Markdown Editor with live split-pane preview.
  - Code syntax highlighting for Python, JS, C++, HTML/CSS.
  - LaTeX Math Formula Rendering (via KaTeX offline) for Physics & Math formulas.
  - Tagging & Search: Fast multi-tag filtering (e.g. #Physics, #Maths, #Algorithms).
  - Export to Markdown, Plain Text, or formatted HTML.

5. ASSIGNMENT & TASK KANBAN BOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features:
  - 3-Column Visual Board: To Do ➔ In Progress ➔ Completed.
  - Priority Badges: Urgent (Coral), Medium (Amber), Normal (Blue).
  - Due Dates with Countdown Badges: "Due in 2 days", "Overdue".
  - Subject tags and checklist subtasks inside each task card.

6. DEVELOPER & UTILITY TOOLBOX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Features:
  - Multi-Mode Calculator: Standard, Scientific, and Programmer (Hex, Dec, Oct, Bin).
  - Unit & World Timezone Converter.
  - Offline QR Code Studio: Generate and export high-res PNG/SVG QR codes.
  - Dev Toolbox: JSON Formatter & Validator, Base64 encoder, SHA-256 hash generator.
  - Password Vault: Zero-knowledge AES-256 local encrypted credentials manager.
